      How this works and how accurate it is

      A text classifier (TF-IDF features with logistic regression) gives the score. Twelve hand-written red-flag rules give the reasons. The final risk is the higher of the two. The model was trained on public English SMS spam and phishing data plus synthetic Ghana-style MoMo messages.

      Tested onScams caughtGenuine messages flagged
      Held-out English SMS95%0.3%
      Real mobile-money SMS from Mozambique (translated), never seen in training93%29%
      40 hand-written Ghana test messages60%15%

      What it cannot do. It only sees the text. A forged "Payment received" alert reads exactly like a real one; the difference is the sender. So a low score does not mean a message is safe. Always check your balance on the official menu. It does not know whether a transaction happened, and it is a research prototype tested on no real Ghanaian scam messages.
