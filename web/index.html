      Someone says they sent you money by mistake?
      1. Check your real balance on the official MoMo menu or app. Don't trust the SMS or a screenshot.
      2. Look at who sent the alert. Real alerts come from the provider's name, never a normal phone number.
      3. Don't send money to a different number, and don't let anyone rush you.
      4. Never share your PIN or a code, and never dial a code someone gives you.

      If the money really arrived, ask your provider to reverse it. Don't send it back yourself.

      How does this work?

      The checker looks for common scam signs, like "sent by mistake", requests for your PIN, threats to block your account and spelling mistakes. A computer model trained on thousands of text messages also scores the wording.

      In testing on real Mobile Money scam messages it caught 93 out of 100. It also wrongly warned about some genuine messages, and it can miss scams.

      Everything runs on this computer. Your message is not uploaded or saved. A "no scam signs" result does not guarantee a message is safe.
