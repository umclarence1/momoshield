      How it works

      Three steps, all on this computer

      1

      Remove personal details

      Phone numbers, amounts, names and transaction IDs are replaced with labels such as <PHONE>, so the check looks at how the message is written, not who it mentions.

      2

      Look for red flags

      Thirteen checks look for known scam signs: "sent by mistake", requests to send money back, threats to block your account, PIN or OTP requests, fees to claim prizes, and spelling or grammar mistakes.

      3

      Score the message

      A machine-learning model trained on thousands of SMS gives a risk score. The final rating is the higher of the model score and the red-flag score.

      Tested onScams caughtGenuine flagged

      What it cannot do

      It only reads the text. A forged "Payment received" alert can be word-for-word identical to a real one; only the sender is different. A low score does not mean a message is safe.

      It is a research prototype and has not been tested on real Ghanaian scam messages. Always check your balance on the official menu before sending money.
