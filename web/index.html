      Methodology

      How the analysis works

      1

      Normalise

      Phone numbers, amounts, names and transaction IDs are replaced with labels, so the analysis looks at how a message is written rather than who it mentions.

      2

      Rule checks

      Fifteen checks for known scam signs: "sent by mistake", requests to return money, account threats, PIN and OTP requests, prize fees, spelling and grammar errors, and official-looking alerts from personal numbers.

      3

      Model score

      A text classifier trained on public SMS datasets and synthetic Ghana-style messages scores the wording. The risk shown is the higher of the model score and the rule score.

      Limitations. A forged alert can match a real one word for word; only the sender differs, so enter the sender when you can. A low score does not mean a message is safe.
